0 - Milestone
    ---------

    The end goal of L002 is to prove mirror unrefutability in M001
    vocabulary. The implementation splits the result into a proof-minimal
    kernel and a public full-interface wrapper.

    The proof-minimal kernel is:

    Theorem mirror_unrefutability_core :
      forall (M : RegulatorTheory)
             (Gamma : Context)
             (chi_eq : DiagonalFormula),
        ChiEqMInternal M Gamma chi_eq ->
        MirrorConsistent M Gamma ->
        ~ regulator_theory_checked_derivable M Gamma
            (formula_negation chi_eq).

    The public full-interface wrapper is:

    Theorem mirror_unrefutability :
      forall (Slambda M : RegulatorTheory)
             (Gamma : Context)
             (chi_eq : DiagonalFormula)
             (code_Slambda_derives : CodingOfSLambdaDerivability),

        (* (H1) External inclusion: every M-checked-derivation
                is a Sλ-checked-derivation. *)
        regulator_theory_included M Slambda ->

        (* (H2) Internal inclusion: for every A, if M
                checked-derives A, then M checked-derives the
                Formula coding "Sλ checked-derives A". *)
        InternalInclusionViaCoding M Gamma code_Slambda_derives ->

        (* (H3a) Coding internal accuracy: if M checked-derives
                 the Formula coding "Sλ checked-derives A", then M
                 checked-derives A itself. *)
        CodingInternalAccuracy M Gamma code_Slambda_derives ->

        (* (H4) Diagonal on chi_eq: Sλ checked-deriving chi_eq is
                equivalent (at the meta-level) to the universal
                equivalence of M-checked-derivability and
                Sλ-checked-derivability. *)
        DiagonalEliminator Slambda M Gamma chi_eq ->

        (* (H6) M-internal Curry property of chi_eq. *)
        ChiEqMInternal M Gamma chi_eq ->

        (* (H5) Consistency of M. *)
        MirrorConsistent M Gamma ->

        (* Conclusion: M cannot checked-derive the negation of
           chi_eq. *)
        ~ regulator_theory_checked_derivable M Gamma
            (formula_negation chi_eq).

    This is the constructive M001-native cousin of the prose
    Mirror Unrefutability Lemma. It uses M001's existing
    checked-derivability vocabulary as the evaluation analogue of
    classical provability, and introduces no arithmetized Bew,
    no HBL machinery, and no Π₁-soundness of M.

    The hypotheses are all explicit. None are smuggled. The two
    load-bearing pieces are `code_Slambda_derives` (which carries the
    role of `Prov_Sλ(⌜A⌝)`) and `chi_eq` (the diagonal Formula).
    Both are Π-quantified parameters in the theorem signature.

    Note on interface revision. The initial five-hypothesis form of
    the milestone did not close the proof: no hypothesis provided an
    M-internal handle on chi_eq's behavior, and `DiagonalEliminator`
    described chi_eq only from Sλ's side. The redesigned interface
    adds (H6), `ChiEqMInternal`, as the M-side Curry property of
    chi_eq. The two diagonal hypotheses (Sλ-side H4, M-side H6)
    describe chi_eq from both sides and are jointly the constructive
    cousin of the classical diagonal lemma.

    Interface revision (second pass). The original `CodingSoundness`
    hypothesis bundled two distinct claims: M-internal accuracy of
    the coding operator, and M-into-Sλ inclusion at the consequences.
    The inclusion direction is already provided by (H1) via M001
    monotonicity; the M-internal accuracy is a separate schema. The
    refactor replaces `CodingSoundness` with `CodingInternalAccuracy`
    and provides a derived lemma
    `coding_soundness_from_accuracy_and_inclusion` that recovers the
    old form. The milestone theorem's statement and proof are
    unchanged in load-bearing structure; only the documentary
    hypothesis name has changed.

    Interface revision (third pass). The theorem layer now separates
    the proof-minimal kernel from the full documentary interface.
    `mirror_unrefutability_core` consumes only `ChiEqMInternal` and
    `MirrorConsistent`. The public theorem `mirror_unrefutability`
    keeps (H1), (H2), (H3a), and (H4) in its signature to document
    that chi_eq is intended to be a full mirror diagonal, but it
    delegates to the core theorem.


1 - Summary
    -------

    L002 formalizes the SYMBOLIC REGULATION layer as a clean
    M001-native mirror-unrefutability package. It works with two
    regulator theories, a shared context, an explicit formula-coding
    operator for Sλ-derivability, and a supplied diagonal formula
    chi_eq.

    L002 is constructive throughout. It does not internalize
    arithmetic provability, does not introduce HBL, does not assume
    Π₁-soundness of the regulator content, and does not assume
    classical excluded middle. The two pieces of the classical
    argument that M001 does not natively provide — a Formula coding
    of Sλ-derivability and a diagonal Formula for the cross-regulator
    equivalence — are Π-quantified parameters of the end-goal
    theorem. Each parameter is a refinement target: future work
    discharges it by construction (e.g., via L001's evaluation
    closure) without changing the theorem's statement.


2 - Provenance
    ----------

    The L002 mathematical content is an M001 checked-derivability
    cousin of Chapter 2 of the Symbolic Regulation document. The
    chapter's stated mirror theorem and its frozen Goal use
    arithmetized provability. M001 refuses that machinery; L002
    carries the relevant structure in checked-derivability terms.

    L001 is a future refinement source, not a dependency of the
    current theorem layer. The current L002 source imports only M001
    through L002_00__Premises.


3 - Intuition
    ---------

    Two regulator theories over a shared context:

      Slambda : RegulatorTheory     -- object-level symbolic regulator Sλ
      M : RegulatorTheory     -- inner (regulator content)
      Gamma : Context

    The structural hypothesis throughout L002 is the M001 axiom-set
    inclusion `regulator_theory_included M Slambda`, from which M001's
    monotonicity lemma `regulator_theory_checked_derivable_
    regulator_theory_monotone_lemma` lifts inner derivations to
    outer derivations.

    Two further pieces are required for the §0 end-goal theorem and
    are not derivable from M001 alone:

      code_Slambda_derives : Formula -> Formula
        A Formula-valued operation whose checked-derivability in M
        is meant to *encode* Sλ-derivability. M001 has no native
        Bew; this operator is the load-bearing parameter that
        plays the role of Prov_Sλ.

      chi_eq : Formula
        The diagonal Formula whose Sλ-derivability tracks the
        universal equivalence of M-derivability and Sλ-derivability.
        M001 has no Diagonal Lemma; this is supplied as a parameter
        and constrained by a meta-level biconditional hypothesis.


3a - Parameter interface
     -------------------

    The end-goal theorem (§0) has six hypotheses. (H1) and (H5)
    are M001-native. (H2), (H3a), (H4), and (H6) are the
    parameter interface:

      (H2)      Internal-inclusion-via-code_Slambda_derives
      (H3a)     Coding internal accuracy of code_Slambda_derives
      (H4)      Diagonal eliminator on chi_eq
      (H6)      M-internal Curry property of chi_eq

    Each is a Π-quantified hypothesis on `mirror_unrefutability`.
    They are explicit and named. No Axiom is introduced.

    Refinement targets:

      code_Slambda_derives :
        Stage 1 - construct via L001's evaluation closure
                  (ClosureEvaluationFrame applied to the relation
                  `λ A. regulator_theory_checked_derivable Slambda Gamma A`).

      chi_eq :
        Stage 2 - construct via L001's evaluation closure at the
                  goal-frame form, applied to the cross-regulator
                  equivalence predicate. May require a closure-
                  level Diagonal Lemma added as a hypothesis in
                  L002_00.

      (H2) :
        Discharge by the same construction that produces
        code_Slambda_derives. The conditions become provable lemmas
        rather than hypotheses.

      (H3-fwd) :
        Now derived. The previous hypothesis `CodingSoundness` is
        recovered from `CodingInternalAccuracy` and
        `regulator_theory_included` via M001's monotonicity lemma;
        the recovery lemma is
        `coding_soundness_from_accuracy_and_inclusion` in
        `L002_00__Premises.v`.

      (H3a) :
        Discharge by the same construction that produces
        `code_Slambda_derives`. Whatever evaluation-closure construction
        produces a Formula coding Sλ-derivability must also satisfy
        the internal accuracy schema; this is the natural property
        of the construction.

      (H4) :
        Discharge by the same construction that produces chi_eq.

      (H6) :
        Discharge by the same construction that produces chi_eq.
        L001's evaluation closure at the goal-frame variant applied
        to formula_negation produces the Curry refutation-to-derivation
        path; future work derives ChiEqMInternal from that construction.

7 - Constructive constraints
    ------------------------

    L002 inherits L001 and M001's constructive commitments:

      - No internalized arithmetic provability predicate.
      - No HBL, no Σ₁ machinery, no arithmetic coding at the M001
        Formula level.
      - No semantic truth predicate beyond what M001 exposes.
      - No Π₁-soundness of M.
      - No classical excluded middle in the L002 metalanguage.

    The §0 end-goal theorem introduces two Π-quantified parameters
    (code_Slambda_derives, chi_eq). Neither is an Axiom; both are
    hypotheses on the theorem, explicit in the signature, and
    refined away in later stages by construction from L001's
    evaluation closure.

    Future extensions may add:

      - A closure-level Diagonal Lemma as a hypothesis in
        L002_00, enabling construction of chi_eq from a generic
        predicate.
      - A Bekić-style modeling layer (M2A, M2B sub-closures with
        mutual photograph operations) as a richer account of
        modeling. Out of scope for the §0 end goal.
      - Object-level LEM as an optional hypothesis to recover the
        classical conclusions of the prose chapter at locations
        where the constructive version is strictly weaker.
